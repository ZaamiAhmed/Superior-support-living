Our Story

We're More Than Caregivers —
We're a Community

Superior Support Living was founded on a simple belief: that people deserve to live with dignity, comfort, and joy — no matter their age or ability.

Rooted in Rochester

Built on Compassion, Made for Families

We started Superior Support Living because we saw a gap — too many families struggling to find a care option that felt genuinely personal. Large facilities can feel impersonal and overwhelming. We set out to do something different.

A real home. With real caregivers. Offering real relationships.

Every decision we make — from how we hire staff to how we design daily routines — comes back to one question: is this what we'd want for our own family?

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Compassionate care in a place that feels like home.

To provide compassionate, personalized care in a warm and supportive home environment — where every resident is treated with dignity, respected as an individual, and supported to live as fully and independently as possible.

Our Vision

A community where everyone belongs.

A Rochester community where every person — regardless of age, ability, or background — has access to a safe, nurturing home where they truly belong. We envision assisted living not as a last resort, but as a choice made with confidence and hope.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These aren't words on a wall — they're the principles that shape every decision, every hire, and every interaction in our home.

01

Dignity First

Every resident is treated as a whole person — with a history, preferences, and a personality worth knowing and respecting.

02

Transparency

Families are always in the loop. No surprises, no runarounds — just honest, open communication at every step.

03

Genuine Warmth

We hire for heart. Our caregivers don't just clock in and clock out — they show up with care and presence every single day.

04

Safety & Stability

From medication management to fall prevention, every system we have is built to give residents and families peace of mind.
