Our Story

Rooted in Rochester,
Built on Compassion

We started Superior Support Living because we saw a gap — too many families struggling to find a care option that felt genuinely personal. Large facilities can feel impersonal and overwhelming. We set out to do something different: a real home, with real caregivers, offering real relationships.

Every decision we make — from how we hire staff to how we design daily routines — comes back to one question: is this what we'd want for our own family?

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Compassionate care in a place that feels like home.

To provide compassionate, personalized care in a warm and supportive home environment — where every resident is treated with dignity, respected as an individual, and supported to live as fully and independently as possible.

Our Vision

A community where everyone belongs.

A Rochester community where every person — regardless of age, ability, or background — has access to a safe, nurturing home where they truly belong. We envision assisted living not as a last resort, but as a choice made with confidence and hope.
